## Goal and non-negotiable output contract

Convert all 10,327 Falcon-versus-Fox Battlefield Slippi matches and commit them to the user's Google Drive dataset.

- Native 642x528 pixels; no crop, scale, binning, or other spatial transform.
- Exact constant 20 fps H.264/yuv420p.
- No audio.
- Preserve the official Slippi Playback HUD. Do not enable Center Align 2P HUD.
- Upload batches as `.tar.zst`, at most 100 results and 1.5 GiB of source plus result bytes. Prefer at least 20 results per free lane so uploaders do not sit idle.
- Upload each manifest last. Only manifest-backed archives count as committed.
- CPU-only Daytona workers. Optimize for completion speed.
